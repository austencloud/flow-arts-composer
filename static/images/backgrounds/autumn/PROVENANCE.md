# Autumn background plates

## Far plate

`amber-rain-far.webp` and `amber-rain-far-portrait.webp` are crops of Jervis
McEntee, _Autumn in the Catskills_ (1873), oil on canvas, Cleveland Museum of
Art.

- License: public domain (Wikimedia Commons; the museum's Open Access images
  are CC0)
- Source page: https://clevelandart.org/art/430.1915
- File used: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Jervis_McEntee_-_Autumn_in_the_Catskills_-_430.1915_-_Cleveland_Museum_of_Art.tiff
  (4620 × 5496 TIFF)
- Processing: 1.5% canvas edge trimmed; wide plate is a 16:9 band 40% of the
  way down, portrait plate a 9:16 column centred at 47% across; soft-light
  `#c2551a` at 55%, then brightness 80% and saturation 108%; 0.8 px Gaussian
  blur; WebP quality 78.

It replaced an AI-generated far plate whose repeated trees read as generated
(Austen, 2026-10-26).

## Middle and near plates

`amber-rain-middle*.webp` and `amber-rain-near*.webp` are the original
project-made layers and are unchanged.

## Flat fallbacks

`amber-rain-grove.webp` and `amber-rain-grove-portrait.webp` are the far,
middle and near plates stacked in that order (WebP quality 80). The renderer
uses them at minimal quality, so rebuild them whenever a plate changes.
