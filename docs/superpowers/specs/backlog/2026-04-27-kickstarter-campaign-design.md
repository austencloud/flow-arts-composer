# Kickstarter Campaign Design: TKA Choreo Cards

**Date:** 2026-04-27
**Status:** Draft. Awaiting content production (photos, video) before launch.

---

## Overview

Kickstarter campaign to fund professional printing of TKA Choreo Card decks. Each deck contains the complete mathematical space for a given LOOP configuration. Not a curated selection. Every possible sequence the algorithm produces.

### Timeline

1. **Phase 1 (now):** Laminated proof run of complete decks with QR codes + mandalas. Hand out at festivals, film reactions, create content.
2. **Phase 2 (later):** Launch Kickstarter with real photos/video from Phase 1. Professional printing as the upgrade pitch.

### Campaign Identity

- **Name:** Choreo Cards
- **Tagline:** "Choreography you can hold, scan, and learn."
- **Tone:** Educational resource, not a product pitch. Show the object, explain the system, let the depth sell itself. No SaaS energy, no conversion funnels, no screaming CTAs.

---

## Products

### Tier 1: Complete Decks (Kickstarter products)

Configurations where exhaustive enumeration fits a physical deck. The deck IS the complete space.

| Deck | Configuration | Cards | Concept |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Starter** | L1 halved strict-rotated 4-beat | 47 | Basic LOOP structure |
| **Deep Dive** | L1 quartered strict-rotated 8-beat | 128 | Quartered rotation |
| **VTG Ratio** | L1 VTG motions × 7 ratios | ~133 | Timing relationships |

Additional decks unlocked via stretch goals or future releases.

### Tier 2: Curated Collections (future / ongoing model)

Configurations that produce 1000+ sequences. Hand-picked or algorithmically sampled. Weekly/monthly/seasonal drops. Patreon or subscription model. Not part of the initial Kickstarter.

### The Combinatorial Scale

14 LOOP types × 2 slice sizes × multiple beat lengths × reversal patterns × turn levels × grid modes = effectively infinite deck configurations. Each one a legitimate, complete, playable deck. This is a card game engine, not a card game. The Kickstarter is the first release from an inexhaustible system.

## Campaign Page Structure

### 1. Hero Section (above the fold)

- Full-bleed photo: hands fanning choreo cards, mandala backs visible, one card flipped showing the pictograph front
- Headline: "Choreo Cards: choreography you can hold, scan, and learn"
- Subline: "Each card is a sequence. Scan the QR code. Watch it animate. Learn it with your body."
- Embedded video (60-90 sec)

### 2. The Object (what you're getting)

- Card front/back beauty shots: close-ups of pictograph notation, mandala, QR code
- Photo of someone scanning a QR code → phone showing 3D animation
- Card stock and finish details (vendor-dependent)
- "Every possible sequence in this mathematical space. The complete set."

### 3. VTG Acknowledgment

"TKA was built on the foundation of Vulcan Tech Gospel (VTG), created by Noel Yee and spinners at the Vulcan Lofts in Oakland. VTG gave flow arts its first shared language for timing and direction. TKA takes that foundation and adds a combined notation: both hands described together in a single letter. The VTG categories are still here, built into every card as a classification layer."

### 4. The System (what's behind it)

- Brief TKA explainer: notation system for flow arts, a letter for every movement
- App screenshots: sequence generator, 3D viewer, browse gallery
- The completeness claim: "Each deck contains every possible sequence for its configuration. The complete mathematical space."
- The scale tease: "53,000+ sequences enumerated so far. 14 LOOP types. The space is barely mapped."

### 5. The Decks (what ships)

Each deck gets:
- Name and card count
- What LOOP concept it teaches
- Sample card images (front + back)
- One-sentence description of the concept

### 6. Stretch Goals

Visual roadmap showing unlock tiers:

### 7. About the Creator

"I built TKA because I wanted to write down what my hands were doing. A letter for every movement a staff can make. Letters combined into sequences. Sequences became cards.

The app is free at tkaflowarts.com. The cards are the physical version. Shuffle through them at a festival, hand one to a friend, spread them across a table and study the patterns."

No fabricated biographical claims. No years-of-experience. The work is the credential.

### 8. Risks & Challenges

"Cards are printed by a professional playing card manufacturer. Production takes 2-4 weeks after the campaign closes, plus 2-3 weeks for shipping. Updates with production photos along the way.

The digital infrastructure (QR codes, 3D animations, the app) already exists and is live. The cards make it physical."

## Target Audience

**Primary:** Flow arts community. People who spin staves, fans, clubs. May already know TKA or VTG.

**Secondary:** Adjacent movement disciplines: dancers, martial artists, circus performers, jugglers. Understand body mechanics but don't know TKA notation.

Campaign teaches briefly but doesn't dumb down. Flow community finds depth. Adjacent audience is grabbed by the object.
