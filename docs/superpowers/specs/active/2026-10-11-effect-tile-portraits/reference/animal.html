The creature's head is the prop tip, and its body is the figure the tip just drew: a lead-in from the upper right into a coil the body wraps around, the tail leaving to the left. Short creatures make a hook, medium ones a C, and the longest close the loop so the neck passes over the coil (that patch is redrawn in depth order). The renderer's own build is ported as is: the fixed body length (short Inchworm, long Gilt Wyrm), the snout-to-tail width profile (plump grub vs slender snake), the travelling slither wave ramped from zero at the head to full at the tail (frozen mid-wag), contact shadow, cylinder shading, scale chevrons, the sheen down the lit edge, and each creature's ornaments: snake tongue mid-flick, dragon crest, horns and lagging whiskers (replayed along the path), caterpillar legs, bands and antennae. Ethereal grades body to bodyAlt head to tail; ember adds the aura and composites additively. Each creature is centered and zoomed to fit the frame (capped at 1.45x); widths are boosted 2.8x for tile size, length 1.35x, and intensity gets a floor of 0.62 so Wisp and Whisper are not threads at 304x114.
