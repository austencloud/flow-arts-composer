## Outcome

Celestial becomes a sunlit natural refuge above a deep cloud ocean. The scene
keeps one broad dry limestone performance terrace, two wind-shaped olive trees,
one restrained lagoon at the outer right edge, a far natural sun, and a field
of separated eroded mesas at different elevations.

The terrace is not a small platform floating without context. It is the exposed
front edge of a much larger limestone landmass that continues behind the camera.
A broad natural approach joins that unseen mass to the performance terrace.
The shelf continues beyond the camera frame. This is a fixed celestial location,
so no literal arrival or departure is staged.

## Spatial contract

- The larger landmass continues beyond the rear frame and beneath the camera.
- The approach is at least 8 metres wide and merges directly into the terrace.
- The central performance radius is at least 5.75 metres and stays dry.
- The single lagoon remains outside the performer lane on the right edge.
- The two olive trunks remain outside the clear performance radius.
- At least two distant mesas rise above the main terrace elevation.
- The distant forms are natural limestone mesas, never towers, columns, ruins,
  or fragments of a castle.
- The sun remains far beyond every mesa and acts as the only solar source.

## Visual hierarchy

1. Dry performance terrace.
2. Paired olive trees framing the center.
3. One right-edge lagoon reflecting the sun.
4. Distant raised mesas and thin waterfalls.
5. Far natural sun and surrounding cloud light.
