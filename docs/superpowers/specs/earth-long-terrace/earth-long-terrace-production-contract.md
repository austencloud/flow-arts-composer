## Experience sentence

> The player enters from Fire, turns through a wet root bluff because cool
> daylight leaks around it, crosses a long terrace beside G, H, and I, reveals
> persistent wall traces through proximity, reaches a near stand where all
> three continuous performances fill one composition, and continues forward
> into Air.

## Design position

The performer row is the hero. The canyon supplies scale and daylight. The
traces make the motion relationship visible. Vegetation frames the route and
canyon edges but does not become a fourth subject.

There is no central tree. Opus identified the old tree as a competing hero and
the horseshoe route as a spatial liability. The Long Terrace preserves the
existing room shell and canyon provenance while replacing the interior
experience.

The story-bible phrase "sequential close encounters" remains explicit. This
plan interprets it as three changing nearest reads rather than three isolated
rooms: all performers stay synchronized and visible, while the visitor's
terrace position makes G, then H, then I closest. Austen and Opus must accept or
reject that interpretation before Gate 1 can pass.

**Cave-wide grammar constraint (2026-08-09, Codex review accepted by Austen —
tracker `RGJjXOrkEJa2F5tqDNTd` and the Earth application below): make the
visitor earn the room's transformation, not every letter, and do not let
Water, Fire and Earth all read as "three solos, then ensemble."** For Earth
concretely: close reads (windows, nearest positions) are optional richness,
never a required or unlockable set; the ensemble composition must be reachable
and complete regardless of how many close reads the visitor took; no
collection, unlock, or three-stop-checklist mechanics anywhere in the room.
Earth's verb is COMPARE — G and H explaining I through simultaneous
comparison — which distinguishes it from Water (choose one) and Fire (complete
the set, the one room whose causal structure earns it). Any Gate 1 candidate
whose route only teaches when all three solos are completed fails this
constraint by construction.
