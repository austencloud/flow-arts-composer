Goo · seven looks

A rope of liquid laid along the tip's last sweep, merged with the renderer's own metaball recipe (blur then contrast), so it gets a real gooey edge, a rim band in the palette's edge color, wet specular glints, and drips that hang off the body. Motion emission sets how thick the rope is, ambient emission how much it oozes and drips at rest, surface tension how long a drip necks, viscosity how slowly it falls, clarity how dark the interior stays, intensity the glow.
