Petals · six looks

The leaves are the app's own silhouettes, drawn by the same module the renderer calls (maple, oak and ginkgo from the autumn and gold rosters, monstera and banana for jungle, five-lobed blossoms and loose petals for classic, charred flakes with ember rims for ash). They lie along the tip's last sweep under the renderer's airstream rule: each leaf launched with carry of the tip's speed, that motion bleeding off at the streak rate, then easing into its fall. Intensity sets size, emission sets how many, and the face flutter turns some edge-on.
