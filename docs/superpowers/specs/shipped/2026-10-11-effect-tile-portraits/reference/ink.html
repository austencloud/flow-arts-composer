Ink · three looks

One calligraphic swash laid down by the tip: a loop through the middle of the frame, exiting right. The ribbon is built the renderer's way: width from tip speed (brush lifts when fast, presses when slow), the broad nib held to the stage center (wide on the sweep, hairline toward or away from the center), extra load in the turn, a 1.42x cap on width change, the tail collapsing to a point. Dense ink gets the four-pass pigment body with its wet gloss behind the brush, pigment grain stamped source-atop, and dry-bristle cuts; neon draws additively. Splatter bursts fly off the sharp turns, and Toxic's viscosity lets the oldest ink shatter into droplets.
