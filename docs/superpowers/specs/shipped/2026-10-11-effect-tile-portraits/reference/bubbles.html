Bubbles · six looks

A field of bubbles rising off the tip's last sweep. Each one is the renderer's bubble: a clear film that brightens toward the edge, a hairline rim, a thin-film sheen on the shadowed side, a specular up-left and a soft bounce down-right. Sizes follow the renderer's power law (mostly small, a few big), the big ones have risen furthest, and a couple have just popped into film slivers. Intensity sets size, size jitter adds the big ones, buoyancy lifts the field, emission sets the count. Oil rims shift magenta → cyan → gold with age.
