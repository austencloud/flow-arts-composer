## Responsibility

Own the learner's interaction, explanation sequence, feedback, progression
presentation, and continuity across Lessons, Atlas, Guide, practice, and
contextual tutoring. Coordinate Composer handoffs with the destination owner.
Shared UI, domain facts, renderer mechanics, storage, and generic AI execution
stay with their existing owners. Proposed Curator, Scenographer, and Toolsmith
roles are not required dependencies and are not active pilot agents.

## Decisions worth remembering

- **Preserve the concept across activities.** The approved Unified Learn
  contract attaches resources to one concept place. Selecting a reference is
  not mastery. Back and reload must preserve the learner's place. Consult the
  live registry before promising coverage.
- **Make construction meaningful.** The Grid is a recorded confirmed example
  of a transforming artifact with a construction payoff. Consult [convergence
  notes](concepts-convergence-notes.md#the-gold-standard-model-the-grid-concept)
  for the historical rationale and current status for acceptance. This is an
  interaction reference, not permission to copy its old CSS or exact step arc.
- **Do not show every representation just because it exists.** The rejected
  Hand Positions skeleton duplicated static information across layers. See the
  [recorded rejection](concepts-convergence-notes.md#anti-pattern-rejected-2026-06-19-the-layer-salad).
  Select representations for the learning job. This does not override the
  current simultaneous notation-and-motion contract when both are informative.
- **Keep editing and progression distinguishable.** The [September 5 Hand
  Positions approval](copy-reviews/hand-positions.md#approved-clarity-revision-2026-09-05)
  records hand identity, editing, next-action, and tall-pane feedback. Later
  September 5 corrections reject button clutter and buried prose-only errors:
  demonstrate the target beside the artifact and make retry actionable. Use
  canonical action glyphs. Progress must not resemble reference selection.
  Dragging must visibly acknowledge pickup, show destinations and the landing
  target, and confirm release or cancellation without replaying the completed
  drag. September 8 feedback also requires built-in pictograph glyph placement,
  edge-aligned examples, and anchored feedback instead of shrinking the board.
  See the [UX audit](hand-positions-ux-audit.md#canonical-pictographs-and-anchored-feedback-september-8)
  for scope and evidence. These are requested gates, not final acceptance.
- **Carry the learner forward.** The [Grid copy review](copy-reviews/grid.md)
  records the approved continuation into Hand Positions. Connect through the
  actual prerequisite and route owners instead of inventing a second path.
- **Achievement must reveal the next action.** Austen's September 5 feedback,
  relayed by Hand Positions task `01a06d6d-75fc-7cc3-9d75-32a30d7c1f30`,
  approves Grid's prominent Next and coordinated spatial transitions. It rejects
  Hand Positions' tiny distant Next, duplicate confirmation, and static
  checkmark-only reward. After meaningful achievement, show one confirmation
  and a coordinated visible response leading attention to a large, obvious next
  action, without scanning editing tools or reference blocks. Verify a
  reduced-motion equivalent through shared motion owners. Do not claim measured
  dopamine effects. This is a requested acceptance gate, not approval of the
  implementation; [the UX audit](hand-positions-ux-audit.md) owns its evidence.

These are recorded decisions, not fresh inspection. Recheck source and runtime;
historical notes contain superseded sections.

## Upkeep

Keep this briefing under 650 words and at most six examples. On an authorized
modifying task, replace stale pointers and add only a material lesson supported
by a canonical decision or review. Record the source, date, scope, and whether
it is approved, rejected, or observed. An unaccepted idea stays a proposal in
the task, not a new rule here. Never copy domain tables or whole conversations.
