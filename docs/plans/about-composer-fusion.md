Date: 2026-09-21. This replaces the direction of the rejected Round 02 static
comparison. Austen explicitly wants the rich Composer page and About fused.
The production experience, not another calibration exercise, is the deliverable.

## Non-negotiable outcome

Keep the cosmic background, Fraunces display type, color, live sequence hero,
notation strip, guided construction, prepared generation, tunnel, supported 3D
viewer, and real community gallery. Preserve the sequence handoff from a
visitor's work to its later views, reduced-motion behavior, lazy loading,

Explain TKA alongside this working experience and include Austen's authorship,
2022 origin, authentic performance photograph, grant credit, and useful links.
Do not invent personal motivations, quotations, testimonials, or new features.

## Composition considered before implementation

Both candidates contain every existing Composer demonstration and About fact.

- Product-led: expressive Composer hero with the live player and immediate TKA
  context; a short notation explanation leads into building; the visitor's
  sequence continues through the tunnel and 3D; gallery and a substantial
  creator portrait close the page.
- Creator-led: open on Austen's portrait and project history, then introduce
  notation and the complete Composer experience below it.

Choose product-led. It preserves the opening Austen values and puts something
usable in reach immediately. The creator-led option would delay the live
product and risk presenting a performer portfolio as the primary subject.
The creator still receives a proper visual section, not a thumbnail/footer note.

```text
Composer title + TKA relationship    live player / notation / atmosphere

guided build + generator            existing complete interactive surfaces
the visitor's sequence              tunnel, then supported 3D / scope note
real community gallery              existing browsing and account boundaries
performance portrait                Austen / origin / grant / contact
```
