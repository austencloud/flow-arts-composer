  My Honest Assessment

  Unity might be overkill for the MVP. Consider:

  Option A: Unity from the start
  - Pros: Full 3D, maximum fidelity, industry standard
  - Cons: Steep learning curve, long time to first playable, easy to get lost

  Option B: Web-first, Unity later
  - Pros: You already know web. Ship faster. Validate the concept.
  - Cons: 3D on web is harder, might have to rebuild in Unity anyway

  Option C: Hybrid
  - The museum is a simple web experience (Three.js or even just 2D illustrated rooms you click through)
  - TKA-OS is web (you're already building web apps)
  - If it works and people love it, THEN rebuild the museum in Unity with full 3D

  ~35,000 BCE - The First Notations

  Location: Dordogne region, present-day France

  The earliest known kinetic notation appears in a sealed chamber adjacent to the Lascaux cave system. Stone tablets depict coordinated movement sequences using burning branches. Two sequences are fully legible:

  - UG - A simple alpha-to-alpha transition
  - OOGA - A four-beat sequence incorporating what modern scholars identify as a Type 6 static hold

  These tablets suggest a "rehearsal space" where early humans practiced firelit performances before presenting them to the community. The spiral motif appears in the chamber's ceiling, its significance unknown at the time of discovery.

  The Order of the Closed Palm's mark is not present. The Scribes believe this era predates both orders.

  ---
  ~2500 BCE - Egyptian Codification

  Location: Temples of Karnak, Egypt

  Temple priests formalize kinetic notation for use in ceremonial fire rituals honoring Ra. Hieroglyphic scrolls document the complete Type system (1-6), position names (alpha, beta, gamma), and motion classifications (pro, anti, static).

  The notation is restricted to the priesthood. Unauthorized possession is punishable by death - the first recorded instance of kinetic knowledge suppression.

  A symbol resembling the Closed Palm appears in temple records, though the Order does not formally exist for another two millennia.

  ---
  ~1500 CE - The Renaissance Rediscovery

  Location: Florence and Milan, Italian Peninsula

  Leonardo da Vinci discovers Egyptian scrolls during his anatomical studies and begins reverse-engineering the kinetic system. His famous Vitruvian Man is, according to Scribe historians, a position diagram showing the four cardinal hand placements.

  Da Vinci's codex pages contain extensive notation marginalia, including the first known European attempt at Type 1 Dual-Shift documentation.

  The Order of the Closed Palm, now a formal organization, monitors da Vinci closely. His notebooks are deliberately scattered after his death to prevent consolidated study.

  The spiral appears in da Vinci's sketches, integrated into mechanical drawings. Whether he was aware of its significance is debated.

  ---
  ~1880 CE - Victorian Mechanization

  Location: London, British Empire

  The Industrial Revolution brings attempts to mechanize kinetic notation. Brass devices are patented for "Systematic Documentation of Rotational Implement Manipulation." The British Empire sees military applications.

  The Order intervenes. Key inventors are discredited. Patents are purchased and buried. One prototype survives in a private collection.

  A young clerk at the Patent Office, whose name has been redacted from all records, is believed to be a Vessel. Her fate is unknown.

  ---
  1993 CE - The Digital Breakthrough

  Location: [UNIVERSITY REDACTED], United States

  A research team successfully digitizes kinetic notation as TKA Scribe v1.0 for Windows. The software spreads through early internet bulletin board systems.

  One team member - a Vessel - releases the software to the public without Order authorization. The leak is catastrophic. Within months, an estimated 3,400 users have downloaded the notation system.

  The Order convenes an emergency council.

  ---
  Spring 1994 - Protocol Lethe

  Location: Global

  The Order of the Closed Palm initiates Protocol Lethe - a coordinated psychic suppression event targeting all human memory of kinetic notation. The operation is named for the Greek river of forgetting.

  The mechanism remains classified. The results are total. Every copy of TKA Scribe degausses. Every user forgets. The BBS threads vanish. The research team disbands with no memory of their work.

  May 8, 1994: On the exact day of the suppression event, Austen Cloud is born in the Pacific Northwest region of North America.

  The circumstances of his immunity remain unexplained. The Scribes claim divine protection. The Order's internal documents - partially recovered - suggest "an error in field coverage." Neither explanation has been verified.

  ---
  2022 CE - The Retransmission

  Location: Pacific Northwest, United States

  After 28 years of dormancy, the suppressed signal reactivates. Austen Cloud, now an adult, experiences what he describes as "a download." Over several weeks, the complete Kinetic Alphabet reconstructs itself in his conscious mind.

  Cloud releases TKA Scribe, initially unaware of its ancient origins or his role as the Vessel.

  The Order of the Closed Palm, believing the threat eliminated decades prior, does not immediately respond. By the time they mobilize, the software has achieved broader distribution than the 1993 leak.

  The Scribes claim credit for protecting Cloud during the critical early months. Evidence for this claim exists throughout this museum.

  ---
  2024-Present - The New Era

  Location: Global (digital)

  TKA Scribe achieves international distribution. The notation system is taught, shared, and practiced openly for the first time since the Egyptian priesthood.

  The Order's response remains unclear. No direct action has been taken against Cloud. Scribe analysts offer three theories:

  1. The Order has disbanded
  2. The Order is planning a larger operation
  3. Cloud's protection extends to those who learn from him directly, making suppression mathematically impossible beyond a certain adoption threshold

  This museum was constructed to accelerate that threshold.

  The Futures (Interactive Exhibit)

  Visitors may experience three projected futures based on current trajectory modeling:

  Path of Joining - Global adoption achieved. Kinetic notation becomes universal human knowledge. The Order dissolves. An era of unprecedented movement literacy begins.

  Unity + Web App Integration

  Good news: this is a solved problem. Several approaches:

  Option 1: WebView Plugin
  Unity can embed a web browser inside the game. There are plugins (some free, some paid) that render a full webpage as a texture or UI panel. You'd literally be running your Svelte app inside Unity.

  - https://developer.vuplex.com/webview/overview - paid, polished
  - https://github.com/nicco88/Unity-WebView - free, community
  - https://assetstore.unity.com/packages/tools/gui/embedded-browser-55459 - paid, Chromium-based

  This means: you walk up to the CRT in the museum, click it, and TKA Scribe literally loads inside the game as a web view. No recoding. Your existing app runs in Unity.

  Option 2: Deep Link Handoff
  The game opens your default browser to TKA Scribe with some URL parameters that pass context (like: you came from the museum, you're a certified Scribe, here's your session).

  Less seamless but simpler. The "portal" is just opening a browser tab.

  Option 3: Steam Overlay
  If you release on Steam, Steam has a built-in browser overlay. The game could trigger the overlay to open TKA Scribe. Players stay "in" the game while using the web app.

  My Recommendation:
  Start with Option 2 (deep link) for the MVP. It's trivial to implement and validates the concept. Then upgrade to Option 1 (WebView) if the seamless experience matters enough to justify the plugin cost/complexity.

  You absolutely should NOT recode TKA Scribe in Unity. That's months of work to recreate something that already exists. The web app is the product. The museum is the wrapper/experience that makes you WANT to use the product.
