## Licensing boundary

Creative Commons states that CC BY 4.0 permits copying, redistribution,
adaptation, and commercial use as long as the user provides attribution and
marks changes. The licensor cannot later revoke those permissions while the
terms are followed. It is therefore suitable for public samples when broad
reuse is the goal, but it would also let others redistribute those samples
commercially.

Do not promise the full production mocap library under CC BY or CC BY-SA if
Austen may want to sell or separately license that library. A hybrid boundary
preserves both goals:

- publish the motion-data specification, contact and footfall metadata schema,
  implementation notes, and representative reference clips for reuse with
  attribution;
- keep the complete cleaned, retargeted production library available under a
  separate commercial license;
- obtain performer releases that explicitly cover the intended public and
  commercial uses before publishing any capture involving another person.

The final license language should receive legal review before assets are
released. The grant response only needs to state what will be shared, not choose
the final legal instrument.

Official license reference:

- https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/

## Recommended 450-character angle

3. Commit the grant-funded contribution to documented motion standards,
   metadata, and representative attributed samples.
4. Do not promise the entire commercial-quality mocap library for free.
