# Epic Elevator Pitch Source Map

## Locked prompt

**Portal question:** Project elevator pitch

**Limit:** 225 characters

**Purpose:** State what the player experiences and the idea that makes this
specific game memorable. Do not spend the limited space on the funding request,
equipment, production method, or general praise.

The elevator pitch should keep this direct sentence rhythm. It should not sound

## Verified story facts

The current story bible establishes that:

- the player walks through an abandoned government archive;
- the institution presents the Kinetic Alphabet's fabricated history as fact;
- the history begins in prehistory and moves through later eras;
- the institution became the Bureau of Kinetic Containment and collapsed in
  2003;
- the player learns through exhibits, physical spaces, and optional details;
- the notation and Flow Arts practice underneath the fiction are real;
- the game is a first-person narrative experience, not a training simulator.

These are fact sources, not permission to copy the story bible's prose into the
application.

## Recommended 225-character angle

1. Lead with the player entering an abandoned bureaucratic museum or archive.
2. State that the institution treats the Kinetic Alphabet as something that has
   shaped human history since prehistory.
3. Make clear that the player learns the real notation system while uncovering
   the institution's collapse.

Save these details for later fields:

- friends and community members portraying historical practitioners;
- mocap and 3D locomotion production;
- the proof-of-concept argument for Flow Arts in video games;
- the full reality-bending science-fiction plot;
- the educational design and historical-era structure.

## Wording guardrails

- Use "walking sim" or "first-person narrative game," not "walkable
  simulation."
- Show that the player learns a real system without labeling the game an
  educational product.
- Do not imply that the fictional historical figures are real practitioners.
- Do not say the game is already built in Unreal Engine.
- Do not use generic claims about innovation, immersion, or changing games.

### Revision interview

**Austen, August 30, 2026:**

> Somebody who doesn't know what the kinetic alphabet is would not be able to
> read that you need to frame it like spinning sticks and also make it clear
> that the ultimate end goal of this is Floart's prop choreography comprehension
> and creation and collaboration Or you can play up the dramatic effect of the
> mundanity of the activity against the splendiferousness that it is presented
> with

"Floart's" appears to be a speech transcription of "flow arts." The earlier
fire-jam source already supports the purposes Austen named: understanding the
notation, making sequences, and sharing choreography with other performers. The
January museum session supports the contrast Austen wants because he described
walking around ordinary movement artifacts, taking them "very very seriously,"
and expressing the fabricated history as fact.

### Revised angle awaiting approval

1. Begin with the concrete activity: people spinning sticks or hand-held props.
2. Present that ordinary activity with absurd institutional scale, such as a
   40,000-year bureaucratic history or conspiracy.
3. Land on the real purpose: the player learns a system for understanding,
   creating, and sharing prop choreography.

The short pitch should not open with "Kinetic Alphabet" before giving the reader
a physical image. It may name the system after the image is established. The
institution's collapse can move to the full project details because the 225
characters now need to explain both the activity and the real-world payoff.

The revised angle was approved. Austen approved the replacement 223-character
draft on August 30, 2026. The final text and authorship review are saved in
