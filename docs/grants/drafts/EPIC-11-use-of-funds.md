# Epic MegaGrants: Use of Funds

**Portal prompt:** How do you plan to use the funds for the project? Please
include a high level budget (i.e., $x funding for developer training, hiring UE
engineers, etc)

**Limit:** 15,000 characters

**Approved by Austen:** August 30, 2026

**Portal text:**

I am requesting $75,000 to build the three-room Unreal prototype described
above.

$30,000 will pay for my development time. I will build the Unreal project,
connect it to Flow Arts Composer, and implement the interactive exhibits.

$9,000 will pay for three fixed technical checkpoints with an Unreal animation
specialist: a review of the Motion Matching and Pose Search setup, a Control Rig
contact-correction pass, and retargeting validation on differently proportioned
avatars. Each engagement will have a specific deliverable and stopping point.

$10,000 will cover a Rokoko-based body and finger capture setup, global hand
positioning, lightweight staff tracking, textile sizing, calibration, and
capture accessories.

$8,000 will cover performers, rehearsal time, studio space, and capture
sessions.

$6,000 will cover capture-side cleanup, clip preparation, and labeling for prop
contact, foot support, and musical timing before the motion enters Unreal.

$5,000 will cover software, storage, backups, playtesting, and publication of
the documented motion format and representative sample clips.

$7,000 will remain as a production reserve for retakes, additional textile
sizes, replacement equipment, or one additional specialist checkpoint if the
first capture sessions expose a problem inside the planned scope.

The grant would buy the time and evidence I need to stop approximating movement
by hand. The result will be a playable Unreal prototype connecting a written
Kinetic Alphabet sequence to captured performance.
