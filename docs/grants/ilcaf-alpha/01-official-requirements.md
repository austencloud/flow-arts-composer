# FY27 CAF Official Requirements

## Official sources

- Program page: https://arts.illinois.gov/granting-opportunities/grants-programs/creative-accelerator-fund.html
- FY27 guidelines: https://arts.illinois.gov/content/dam/soi/en/web/arts/sites/default/files/guidelines/IAC_FY27%20Creative%20Accelerator%20Fund%20Guidelines.pdf
- Application portal: https://arts-illinois.my.site.com/

The program page and FY27 guidelines were checked on August 31, 2026.

## Schedule and selection

- Guidelines released: August 20, 2026
- Portal opens: September 2, 2026
- Deadline: October 14, 2026 at 11:59 PM Central Time
- Review and notifications: October through December 2026
- Grant-funded activity may begin no sooner than January 1, 2027; the grant
  period ends August 31, 2027.
- Final report due: September 30, 2027
- Award: $10,000, taxable
- Review first checks eligibility, completeness, and ability to receive State
  of Illinois funds.
- Eligible applications are randomized within regional allocations in
  partnership with the Illinois State Lottery. Artistic quality is not ranked.

## Applicant requirements

An applicant must:

- be an individual artist age 21 or older;
- have established Illinois residency before October 14, 2025;
- have an active and sustained artistic practice dating back to at least 2023;
- not be enrolled in a degree-obtaining program during application or award;
- not have received a FY25 or FY26 CAF award; and
- be eligible to receive State of Illinois funds.

Eligible areas include dance, digital media, film/video, performance,
community-based arts, and teaching artistry. Software development is not an
eligible artistic practice. Austen's application should therefore describe
his performance, choreography, instruction, notation authorship, and media-art
practice. The browser application is supporting evidence, not the occupation
under which he applies.

## Narrative

Submit either:

- one written page; or
- one audio or video response of no more than two minutes.

It must answer:

1. What art form or forms does the applicant practice?
2. In what Illinois community area or areas does the applicant practice?
3. How would the award benefit the applicant's practice?

## Work samples

Two samples are mandatory:

1. One sample created by the applicant in 2024, 2025, or 2026.
2. One sample created by the applicant in 2023 or earlier.

Each sample needs a description of no more than 1,000 characters. The
completion date or year must be stated. Missing a completion date makes the
application ineligible.

Public, no-password links are permitted. Do not use an Instagram-only link or
another link that requires the reviewer to sign in. File uploads must remain
under 25 MB. If a sample used generative AI or another AI-assisted process, the
description must disclose the use and establish meaningful human authorship.

## Illinois identity and residency documents

- The Illinois driver's license or state ID must expire on or after October
  15, 2026.
- If the ID was issued after October 14, 2025, the application also needs
  evidence of at least one year of Illinois residency, such as an older
  Illinois ID, a qualifying utility bill, or a 2024 W-2/1099.
- Identity and tax documents stay out of this repository. Austen supplies them
  directly to the State portal when required.
