## Voice constraints

- Explain flow arts as choreographed movement with handheld props before using
  insider terms.
- Prefer concrete actions: spin, teach, rehearse, draw, film, print, perform.
- Do not call the work revolutionary, seamless, transformative, or a platform
  that unlocks potential.
- Do not overuse punchy fragments or slogan structures.
- Keep Austen's delight in systems, discovery, and preservation.
- Preserve uncertainty where Austen is still deciding. Do not convert a wish
  into a promised deliverable.
