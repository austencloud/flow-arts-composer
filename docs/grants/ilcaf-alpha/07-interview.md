# Focused CAF Interview

Answer these in ordinary speech. Fragments, profanity, detours, and uncertainty
are useful. They preserve Austen's voice better than polished grant language.

## 1. The practice

If you had to introduce yourself to another Illinois artist without mentioning
the app or your day job, what would you say you make and do?

What do you actually spend your art time doing in a normal month: performing,
rehearsing, teaching, inventing sequences, drawing notation, filming, hosting
jams, or something else?

Which label feels most truthful as the lead: flow artist and choreographer,
performance artist, teaching artist, media artist, or a combination?

## 2. Illinois community

What did Taco Tuesday look like when it began, what was your role, and what
does it look like now?

Which Chicago or Illinois communities do you consider yourself accountable to?
Name the places, groups, and people you actually work with.

Tell one recent story that shows the practice affecting another person. It
could be a workshop moment, a collaboration, a performance, or somebody using
the notation without you in the room.

## 3. What $10,000 changes

Imagine the award arrives in January 2027. By August, what do you want to have
done that you cannot realistically do at your current pace?

How much of the award needs to pay you for time? What work would that time
replace or make possible?

Which expenses belong to the art practice itself: performers, rehearsal space,
capture equipment, filming, editing, printing, travel, festival fees, or
something else?

What would still happen without the award, and what would only happen because
of it?

## 4. Recent sample

For `Kinetic Fire 2024 - Words with Friends`, who came up with the activity,
what did you teach, what did you perform, who filmed and edited it, and who are
the other performers?

Do you have their permission to use the video in a grant application?

Did any AI system generate or materially alter part of the video, titles,
audio, choreography, or notation?

## 5. Older sample

For the November 2023 Level 1 Guide, what did you personally write, draw,
design, test, and revise?

Which prior systems or collaborators need credit for concepts that appear in
the guide?

Did any AI system generate or materially alter part of that edition?

Would you rather submit the complete 80-page guide or a shorter excerpt that a
reviewer can understand quickly?

## 6. Eligibility confirmations

- Are you at least 21?
- Were you an Illinois resident before October 14, 2025?
- Does your Illinois ID expire on or after October 15, 2026?
- What is the ID issue date?
- Are you enrolled in any degree-obtaining program?
- Have you ever received a Creative Accelerator Fund award?

Do not paste an ID number, Social Security number, address, or banking detail
into this file or chat.
