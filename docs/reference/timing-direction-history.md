# Timing and Direction: Historical Provenance and Source Archive

- **Research date:** September 4, 2026
- **Scope:** The six two-prop timing-and-direction modes, their public documentary
  history, VTG/FAI codification, TKA's placement vocabulary, and the 2026
  VTG4/SpiroAnim release.
- **Status:** Evidence ledger, not an inventorship ruling.

## Executive finding

The six-mode model is best understood as a Cartesian product:

- **Timing:** together (0 degrees), quarter (90 or 270 degrees), or split (180 degrees)
- **Direction:** same or opposite

That produces Together/Same, Together/Opposite, Split/Same, Split/Opposite,
Quarter/Same, and Quarter/Opposite.

The public record found in this audit supports four conclusions:

1. The original four timing-and-direction combinations were being discussed as
   a complete set in the poi community by August 2002, years before VTG.
2. The phrase **quarter time** appears in a public poi discussion in November
   2002. By 2005, community members were explicitly arguing that timing and
   direction should be treated as separate variables because quarter-time
   weaves and butterflies otherwise break the four-label vocabulary.
3. Noel Yee's May 2009 transition article covers the original four modes and
   calls itself a “synthesis from many sources.” Noel's site dates the original
   VTG chapter to 2010. The evidence therefore supports describing VTG as a
   major codification and teaching lineage, not as the origin of every timing
   and direction relationship it contains.
4. TKA publicly encoded quarter/same and quarter/opposite, while keeping
   placement, timing, and direction in separate fields, no later than its
   June 20, 2025 public Git history. SpiroAnim's quarter work and VTG4 branding
   entered its public Git history in July and August 2026.

The earliest public source found is not necessarily the first person to discover
or teach an idea. Much of flow-arts knowledge circulated in person and on media
that is no longer indexed. These dates establish **public prior art and documented
usage**, not private priority or sole authorship.

## The complete six-mode model

This table was checked against the Flow Arts Knowledge MCP on the research date.
The TKA letter ranges are included to show the current implementation, not to
claim that TKA originated the underlying motions.

| Code | Mode | Phase relationship | Direction relationship | TKA letters | Historical status |
|---|---|---:|---|---|---|
| TS | Together Time, Same Direction | 0 degrees | Same | G, H, I | One of the original four; public pre-VTG evidence |
| TO | Together Time, Opposite Direction | 0 degrees | Opposite | D, E, F | One of the original four; public pre-VTG evidence |
| SS | Split Time, Same Direction | 180 degrees | Same | A, B, C | One of the original four; public pre-VTG evidence |
| SO | Split Time, Opposite Direction | 180 degrees | Opposite | J, K, L | One of the original four; public pre-VTG evidence |
| QS | Quarter Time, Same Direction | 90/270 degrees | Same | S, T, U, V | Community extension; public quarter-time evidence predates VTG |
| QO | Quarter Time, Opposite Direction | 90/270 degrees | Opposite | M, N, O, P, Q, R | Community extension; public quarter-time evidence predates VTG |

### Placement is a separate axis

TKA uses Greek letters for the relationship between hand positions:

| Placement | Hand-position relationship |
|---|---|
| beta | Same grid point; 0 degrees apart |
| gamma | Adjacent grid points; 90 degrees apart |
| alpha | Opposite grid points; 180 degrees apart |

This distinction matters because the same angle can describe different things.
**Gamma placement** means the hands occupy adjacent positions. **Quarter timing**
means the props are one quarter-cycle out of phase. Neither entails the other.
Calling both “quarter” collapses two independent variables and makes a complete
motion description less precise.

## Documentary timeline

### 2002: the four relationships and quarter time are already public

- **August 28, 2002:** In Home of Poi's
  [“How do you define a weave?”](https://www.homeofpoi.com/en/community/forums/topics/120838/How-do-you-define-a-weave),
  participants distinguish split versus non-split timing and same versus
  opposite direction. The discussion refers to four timing/direction
  possibilities and applies them to weaves, butterflies, and parallel patterns.
  This is evidence for the full 2-by-2 conceptual space, even though later
  abbreviations such as TS, TO, SS, and SO are not used.
- **November 12, 2002:** Jez names
  [“quarter time butterflies”](https://www.homeofpoi.com/en/community/forums/topics/122222/Concept-moves-and-why-make-lists)
  while discussing concept moves and plane transitions. This is the earliest
  explicit public use of *quarter time* located in this audit.

### 2003: quarter time is used as practical instruction

- **February 12, 2003:** In
  [“One handed BF to the side”](https://www.homeofpoi.com/en/community/forums/topics/123677/One-handed-BF-to-the-side),
  Jez recommends trying the motion in quarter time and describes the resulting
  side-to-side and vertical relationship. Quarter time is being used as shared
  vocabulary, not introduced as a new theory.

### 2005: timing and direction are explicitly separated

- **August 26–27, 2005:** In
  [“Can you do this move?”](https://www.homeofpoi.com/fr/community/forums/topics/611260/1/Re-Can-you-do-this-move),
  TheWibbler states that split timing is independent of direction and maps the
  four combinations to familiar moves:
  regular weave as Split/Same, butterfly as Together/Opposite, parallel weave as
  Together/Same, and split-time butterfly as Split/Opposite.
- In the same thread, Coleman reports an older four-name vocabulary attributed
  to Drew: parallel-time, follow-time, same-time, and split-time. This is useful
  lineage testimony, but the earlier list itself was not independently located,
  so it should remain attributed recollection rather than a verified date.
- Spiralx argues for keeping timing and direction as separate axes specifically
  because quarter-time weaves and butterflies would otherwise require still more
  compound names. TheWibbler then restates all four exact combinations. This is
  strong public evidence for the conceptual architecture later used by TKA.
- **December 3, 2005:** In
  [“degrees”](https://www.homeofpoi.com/fr/community/forums/topics/661099/degrees),
  Rev explains that quarter time describes the props' timing, whereas the angle
  at which a same-time move is performed is spatial. This is an early direct
  statement of the timing-versus-placement distinction.

### 2009: Noel publishes a synthesis; phase language becomes formal

- **May 6, 2009:** Noel's
  [“Prop Transitioning Article”](https://www.homeofpoi.com/fr/community/forums/topics/887635/X-Post-Prop-Transitioning-Article)
  defines together/split as timing and same/opposite as direction, then traverses
  transitions among all four original modes. Noel explicitly calls the article a
  “synthesis from many sources.” This is primary evidence that his teaching work
  was consolidating community knowledge.
- **June 12, 2009:** AlienJon's
  [“Poi Theory of Everything”](https://www.homeofpoi.com/us/community/forums/topics/886966/Poi-Theory-of-Everything-An-ongoing-collaboration)
  defines 0 degrees as in-phase, 180 degrees as antiphase, and 90 degrees as
  quarter time. It also notes that the 90-degree relationship has a leader and
  follower, and applies the model beyond circles to pendulums, stalls, and CAPs.
- **November 6, 2009:** The Home of Poi
  [Vulcan Videos thread](https://www.homeofpoi.com/us/community/forums/topics/921800/4/New-NOELTECH-7-FAN-FOLDING-WITH-COREY)
  records Noel's video **NOELTECH #6: Quarter-Whatever w/ Brain** and preserves
  its [YouTube identifier](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=0S88pJS3Co4).
  Earlier entries in the same thread, dated May 2009, separately list videos for
  Together/Same, Together/Opposite, Split/Same, and Split/Opposite.
- **November 16–20, 2009:** The
  [“In Depth Move Families”](https://www.homeofpoi.com/us/community/forums/topics/899386/In-Depth-Move-Families)
  discussion lists the original four combinations, then formalizes together as
  0 degrees, split as 180 degrees, and quarter as 90 degrees. It treats direction
  independently and explains how same-direction and opposite-direction quarter
  phase produce different geometries. The author suggests *Phase and Symmetry*
  as a more general description of Timing and Direction.

### 2010 onward: VTG and FAI organize the original four

- Noel's [VTG 1 history page](https://noelyee.com/vulcan-tech-gospel-1-vtg-1-2011/)
  says the original chapter was created in 2010, later re-released, and changed
  through community collaboration and evolving nomenclature.
- Noel's current
  [Vulcan Tech Gospel overview](https://noelyee.com/instruction/vulcan-tech-gospel/)
  teaches the four original timing/direction modes and transitions among them.
  It should be read as a strong lineage source for VTG's formal system.
- The Flow Arts Institute's
  [Timing and Direction overview](https://flowartsinstitute.com/tutsday-timing-direction/)
  defines only two timings, together and split, and two directions, same and
  opposite. Its assumptions require props to start together or 180 degrees apart,
  so quarter timing is excluded by the article's premise.
- FAI also has dedicated pages for
  [Together/Same](https://flowartsinstitute.com/portfolio-item/together-time-same-direction/),
  [Together/Opposite](https://flowartsinstitute.com/portfolio-item/together-time-opposite-direction/),
  [Split/Same](https://flowartsinstitute.com/portfolio-item/split-time-same-direction/),
  and [Split/Opposite](https://flowartsinstitute.com/portfolio-item/split-time-opposite-direction/).
  No dedicated FAI Quarter/Same or Quarter/Opposite page was located in this
  audit. That is a bounded search result, not a claim that none has ever existed.
- **November 16, 2011:** Drex's
  [“Third-order quarter time chase”](https://drexfactor.com/weirdscience/2011/11/16/drexs_tech_poi_blog_209_third_order_quarter_time_chase)
  describes a motion phasing between quarter time and Split/Same. This supplies
  another dated, independent quarter-time teaching record between early community
  discussions and the modern TKA/VTG4 implementations.

### 2025: TKA's public data independently encodes placement, timing, and direction

- TKA's public Git history begins with commit
  [`50bdc56`](https://github.com/austencloud/tka-platform/commit/50bdc56725b54fbb363224b8f9f001228b988e02)
  on **June 20, 2025**.
- In that immutable commit,
  [`DiamondPictographDataframe.csv`](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/austencloud/tka-platform/50bdc56725b54fbb363224b8f9f001228b988e02/tka-desktop/data/DiamondPictographDataframe.csv)
  has distinct `start_pos`, `end_pos`, `timing`, and `direction` columns. Its
  gamma-position rows include both `quarter,opp` and `quarter,same` records.
- This establishes a conservative public date by which TKA had operationalized
  placement and timing as independent fields and incorporated QS and QO into its
  letter system. It does not establish the date of Austen's earlier private work.

### August 29, 2026: the public launch contains two materially different claims

The caption for the
[VTG Crew Instagram post](https://www.instagram.com/vulcantechgospel/p/DcoQATwFrUA/)
dates it to August 29, 2026. The public post and Mentive’s reply were both read
directly on September 8, 2026, following the initial September 4 audit.
The post says Mentive “has created the next chapter in VTG history” and calls
quarter placement and timing one of the new chapter's “codes cracked.”

In [his reply](https://www.instagram.com/p/DcoQATwFrUA/c/17953419501233426/),
Mentive disclaims invention of the concepts and describes his contribution as
defining, exploring, and visualizing topics taught by other people. The comment
was initially supplied as a transcript by Austen; its text and August 29
timestamp have now been independently verified on the public post.

The [VTG release-history dossier](vtg-release-history.md) records the original
VTG 3 document and apps, the February 2026 phone-app interruption, and the
August 2026 announcements linking the VTG 3 grid to SpiroAnim and VTG 4.

The fairest reading is that the launch caption claims a new **chapter,
integration, and application**, while Mentive expressly disclaims invention of
the underlying concepts. The promotional phrase “codes cracked” creates an
attribution ambiguity, but the paired record does not support alleging that
Mentive personally claimed to invent quarter timing.

## Mode-by-mode provenance dossiers

### Together Time, Same Direction (TS)

- **Definition:** The props rotate in the same direction with zero phase offset.
- **Pre-VTG evidence:** The August 2002 weave discussion distinguishes non-split
  timing in same direction; the August 2005 thread maps a parallel weave to this
  exact combination.
- **Noel/VTG evidence:** Noel's May 2009 synthesis and video index treat it as one
  of the four standard modes; VTG later systematizes its flower transitions.
- **FAI coverage:** Dedicated page exists.

### Together Time, Opposite Direction (TO)

- **Definition:** The props rotate in opposite directions with zero phase offset.
- **Pre-VTG evidence:** Butterflies appear in the August 2002 discussion; the
  August 2005 thread explicitly maps a regular butterfly to this combination.
- **Noel/VTG evidence:** Present in Noel's May 2009 synthesis and later VTG.
- **FAI coverage:** Dedicated page exists.

### Split Time, Same Direction (SS)

- **Definition:** The props rotate in the same direction 180 degrees out of phase.
- **Pre-VTG evidence:** Split-time, same-direction weave language appears in the
  August 2002 discussion; the August 2005 thread maps a regular three-beat weave
  to the exact combination.
- **Noel/VTG evidence:** Present in Noel's May 2009 synthesis and later VTG.
- **FAI coverage:** Dedicated page exists.

### Split Time, Opposite Direction (SO)

- **Definition:** The props rotate in opposite directions 180 degrees out of phase.
- **Pre-VTG evidence:** The August 2002 discussion recognizes the possibility;
  the August 2005 thread maps a split-time butterfly to the exact combination.
- **Noel/VTG evidence:** Present in Noel's May 2009 synthesis and later VTG.
- **FAI coverage:** Dedicated page exists.

### Quarter Time, Same Direction (QS)

- **Definition:** The props rotate in the same direction 90 or 270 degrees out of
  phase. The offset has chirality because either prop can lead.
- **Pre-VTG evidence:** Quarter-time language is public in 2002 and 2003. The
  August 2005 naming debate specifically uses quarter-time weaves as a reason to
  separate timing from direction. The November 2009 phase discussion describes
  the same-direction quarter relationship geometrically.
- **TKA evidence:** Explicit `quarter,same` records are public by June 20, 2025.
- **FAI coverage:** No dedicated page located.

### Quarter Time, Opposite Direction (QO)

- **Definition:** The props rotate in opposite directions 90 or 270 degrees out
  of phase. The offset has chirality because either prop can lead.
- **Pre-VTG evidence:** Quarter-time butterflies are named in November 2002; the
  August 2005 naming debate again treats them as a case requiring independent
  timing and direction. The November 2009 phase discussion describes the
  opposite-direction quarter relationship geometrically.
- **TKA evidence:** Explicit `quarter,opp` records are public by June 20, 2025.
- **FAI coverage:** No dedicated page located.

## What can be claimed safely

### Strong, source-backed claims

- Public poi discussions used the concept of four timing/direction combinations
  by 2002.
- The phrase *quarter time* appears in a public poi discussion by November 2002.
- Community members explicitly separated timing from direction by 2005.
- Noel publicly described his 2009 transition framework as a synthesis.
- Noel's site dates the original VTG chapter to 2010.
- TKA's public repository independently stores placement, timing, and direction
  and contains QS/QO records by June 20, 2025.
- SpiroAnim's public quarter-mode work began in July 2026, explicitly compared
  itself with TKA in August, and adopted VTG4 branding later that month.
- Mentive expressly disclaimed novelty and personal credit in the supplied launch
  comment transcript.

### Claims this evidence does not establish

- Who first discovered quarter timing in private or in person.
- That any one person invented the original four combinations.
- That Mentive copied implementation code or knowingly concealed prior work.
- That Noel personally wrote the VTG Crew caption, unless account authorship is
  separately established.
- That generative AI produced Mentive's definitions or filled theoretical gaps.
  No primary evidence for that assertion was found in this audit.

## Publishing opportunity

The current search landscape is structurally incomplete. FAI's overview defines
a 2-by-2 model and its four child pages mirror that model. A complete TKA
publication can instead teach the 3-by-2 system while being more precise about
placement, timing, direction, phase, and lineage.

Exact-name searches on September 4, 2026 found a clear split:

- Searches for the four original modes surfaced FAI, Flowarts.io, course pages,
  and the current
  [Wikipedia “Poi definitions” article](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Poi_definitions),
  which still says there are four major categories and cites Noel/FAI.
- Searches for Quarter/Same and Quarter/Opposite surfaced scattered 2009–2011
  forum and DrexFactor material, but no strong dedicated definition page for
  either six-mode category. The exact query did not surface a dedicated FAI
  quarter-mode page.

That makes the quarter pages the clearest content gap. The original four remain
worth publishing because a coherent six-page set, linked to a stronger hub and
interactive visuals, answers the subject more completely than disconnected
tutorial pages.

Recommended information architecture:

1. **Timing and Direction in Flow Arts:** canonical hub explaining the three
   timing states, two direction states, six combinations, and placement as an
   independent axis.
2. One substantive page for each of TS, TO, SS, SO, QS, and QO.
3. A seventh history/attribution page derived from this evidence ledger.

Each mode page should include a plain-language definition, phase diagram,
animation, recognizable examples, leader/follower behavior where applicable,
transitions in and out, aliases, common confusions, and cited history. The four
original pages can surpass FAI through depth and current visualization. QS and
QO can occupy search territory for which no dedicated FAI page was located.

The defensible positioning is not “TKA invented quarter time.” It is:

> TKA gives the complete six timing-and-direction modes a consistent visual and
> symbolic language, while keeping placement independent and documenting the
> community lineage that made the model possible.
