# VTG 3 and VTG 4: documents, apps and announcements

Reviewed September 8, 2026. This dossier supports the VTG history entry and
corrects its former claim that VTG 3 was never published. The evidence includes
a public draft, a released phone app, a working web app, and the VTG Crew’s
Facebook and Instagram announcements.

## What the grid represents

The original VTG 3 document places six movement snapshots on each axis of a
six-by-six grid. Each cell describes a combination using the timing and direction
of the hands/arms first, then the props. Grids are supplied for 1:1, 1:3 and 1:5.
It would be inaccurate to describe the literal axes as a list of hand timing and
direction modes versus a list of prop timing and direction modes: the axes are
snapshots, and the paired relationships label the resulting patterns.

SpiroAnim’s August 27 announcement explicitly connects its grid to VTG 3.
VTG 4 adds coverage of quarter timing and quarter placement, among other
extensions. That dates an application and framework expansion; it does not date
the invention of those concepts. See the broader
[timing and direction history](timing-direction-history.md) for earlier usage.

## Publication and access limits

- The original Google Drive file is titled `VTG3Draft1.pdf`. Its metadata gives
  March 28, 2019 as creation and May 31, 2019 as modification. Those are file
  timestamps, not proof of the first public distribution date. Preserve the
  draft designation; do not infer either a finished book or non-publication.
- The official [Instagram profile](https://www.instagram.com/vulcantechgospel/)
  links SpiroAnim as VTG #4 and Pattern Generator, the original VTG #3 document,
  and the VTG web app. These links were opened during this review.
- The public Facebook feed and the relevant Instagram posts, captions and
  reply were read without signing in. The review did not retrieve every
  historical post or establish that Facebook mirrored the VTG 4 launch.
- The [February 2026 Facebook app-status post](https://www.facebook.com/vulcantechgospel/posts/pfbid02XdNRd2bER7FomfsHfwWwxexWpNc95y7sAjMpMpKtwyYJti3Awo7tS4WeoueekQ38l)
  is research context for app availability, not a historical milestone. It is
  retained here rather than in the public entry or chronology: a temporary
  maintenance problem does not explain the development of the system.
  The Android store listing remains useful evidence of its release, even if
  installation or compatibility later changed. No purchase or installation
  was performed.
- Mentive’s reply was unavailable to the September 4 audit except as a supplied
  transcript. Direct retrieval on September 8 resolves that specific evidence
  gap; the earlier timing-and-direction dossier has been updated accordingly.

## Editorial constraints

Retain the original VTG 1 chapter bylines. Credit the VTG 3 app to MCP and Yee,
and the VTG 4 software to Mentive, alongside the sources’ conceptual credits.
Do not turn an app interruption into an unpublished-work claim, a draft label
into a missing-work claim, or a new numbered installment into an invention claim.
Descriptions of the grid should explain its structure without ranking its
usability against other systems.
