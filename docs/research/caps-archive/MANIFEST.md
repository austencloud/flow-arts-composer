# CAP Origin-Thread Image Archive

Archived 2026-07-19 from the Home of Poi thread **"What are CAP's?"**
(https://www.homeofpoi.com/en/community/forums/topics/891193/What-are-CAP-s, posts ~17
years old / ca. 2009). All links in the thread were live at archive time; downloaded
directly from original hosts. Purpose: research + candidate assets for the `/notation/caps`
destination (spec: `docs/superpowers/specs/archived/2026-07-19-notation-caps-destination-design.md`).

Two distinct sources — credit separately:

Notation key (Zaltymbunk's): `θ1 θ2 ; ρ1 ρ2 ; d` — arm turns, prop turns (negative =
antispin), arm radius, prop radius (wrap fractions), division of the full cycle used.
