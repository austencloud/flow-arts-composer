# Flow Fest polish: round 2, the fire jam conversation

Austen asked for a real conversation about the fire jam before it is built.
These are its questions. The recommended option is first; free-text answers
are welcome on any of them and expected where marked. Answer with the number
and letter (for example `1a 2a 3c 4a 5d 6a 7a 8a`) and add words wherever
the options miss the truth. Questions 1 to 6 shape the Jam run (wave 2) and
Jam 2 (wave 4); 7 shapes the Crowd run (wave 3); 8 corrects the Structures
run (wave 1, already launched with its defaults).

## 1. The circle on Thursday night: how big, and how many? (Jam)

The interview sizes the roped amoeba at about 51 by 32 m, centred near
(90.5, -119.5), entrance at the north-west. The game shows the first night.

- **a. Full size, Thursday crowd (Recommended).** The rope as the interview
  sizes it; 20 to 30 people inside at the peak (about 22:30), 60 to 90
  around the edge sitting and standing; thinning after 01:00. Reads as a
  jam from the review camera without being a Saturday crowd.
- **b. Smaller Thursday rope.** A tighter amoeba (about 35 by 25 m) because
  Thursday is the quiet night; 10 to 15 inside, 30 to 50 around. Truer if
  the rope is laid later in the weekend.
- **c. Full size, full house.** 40 or more inside, 120 or more around.
  Costs people the frame budget does not have unless they are cheap
  instanced spectators; fine for the look if they are.
- **d. Free text.** Say what Thursday actually looks like at 22:30.

## 2. What is in people's hands inside the circle? (Jam, Jam 2)

- **a. Mostly fire after dark (Recommended).** About 60 percent fire props
  (poi, staff, hoop, fans, dragon staff, rope dart, fire clubs), 25 percent
  LED, 15 percent unlit; jugglers about one in eight of those inside, with
  two or three fire clubs each. The 71 authored prop variants already have
  fire and LED finishes.
- **b. Half and half.** Fire and LED in equal measure, more LED early in
  the evening and more fire late.
- **c. Mostly LED on Thursday.** Fire builds through the weekend; Thursday
  is LED poi, hoops and levi-wands with a few fire spinners.
- **d. Free text.** Name the props that are actually common there.

## 3. What does a safety station look like? (Jam)

Austen: "a camping chair in front of a safety towel that might have an
eternal flame but might not."

- **a. Chair, towel, sometimes a flame (Recommended).** A camping chair
  facing into the circle on the rope line, a dark duvetyne towel draped
  over its back or folded on the ground beside it, and at every second or
  third station a small fuel-can flame on the ground in front; a safety
  person in the chair at about half the stations.
- **b. Chair and towel only.** No flames at the stations; flames only at
  the dip station.
- **c. Free text.** Describe one station exactly: where the towel sits,
  what the flame is in, whether there is a bucket.

## 4. Light beyond the LED rope and the props? (Jam, Night)

- **a. None at the circle (Recommended).** The rope on the ground, the fire
  on the props, the moon until midnight, and nothing else; the dip station
  has one work light and the showcase stage its own lights, both outside
  the rope and dim from inside it.
- **b. A few string lights.** Warm string lights on the stage and along the
  Vendor Village path, visible from the circle as distant warmth.
- **c. Floodlights.** A work light or two on stands at the entrance and
  the dip station bright enough to light the near edge of the circle.
- **d. Free text.** What colour and behaviour is the LED rope (steady
  single colour, slow fade, chasing), and is there anything else lit?

## 5. Music at the circle? (Jam, Sound)

- **a. A DJ at the stage edge (Recommended).** Speakers on the showcase
  stage facing the circle, electronic music with a steady beat heard across
  Middle Earth, bass felt from the camps, running until about 02:00.
- **b. A drum circle.** Hand drums at the edge of the circle, no PA.
- **c. Stage only.** Music at the stage for its own crowd; the circle has
  whatever drifts over.
- **d. Free text.** Who plays, from where, how loud, until when.

## 6. Jugglers: where they are and what they do (Juggling, Jam, Jam 2)

- **a. Tent by day, circle by night (Recommended).** The juggling tent is
  where jugglers practise and pass all afternoon (solo cascades, a 4-count
  pair, a three-person feed); after dark two to four of them are inside
  the circle with fire clubs, and one passing pair sets up just outside
  the rope near the tent side. Some juggle while a partner spins beside
  them.
- **b. Tent only.** Jugglers stay at the tent and the stage; the circle is
  for spinners.
- **c. Free text.** What passing patterns actually happen there (4-count,
  3-count, feeds, rings), how many clubs, fire or not, and what the juggling
  tent itself is (a big-top, a frame tent with open sides, a shade canopy).

## 7. Four hundred people: where do they camp? (Crowd)

The interview gives the rule (car camping inside the loop; otherwise tents
by the tree lines and the car at the top parking) but not the split.

- **a. Loop-heavy (Recommended).** About 45 percent car camping in the loop
  interior, 38 percent tents around the lower loop (the inner band and
  across the road by the tree line) with cars at the top, 17 percent at the
  upper tree lines and clearing edges. About 165 camps, 175 cars: 75 in
  the loop, 100 at the top pad and overflow field.
- **b. Mostly car camping.** 60 percent in the loop interior, which packs
  the loop tight and leaves the top lot half empty.
- **c. Mostly tents.** 30 percent car camping; the lower tree line is a
  continuous tent band and the top lot fills.
- **d. Free text.** Say where you would walk to find most people at 17:00
  on Thursday, and whether the lower campground is full by Thursday night.

## 8. Which real structures matter most, and what do they look like? (Structures)

The Structures run is building all of the interview's pins with plain
defaults (sizes from the orthophoto footprints, materials guessed: weathered
grey wood, metal roofs, white canvas). Your answer corrects it in wave 3.

- **a. Middle Earth first, everything else plain (Recommended).** Spend the
  detail on the showcase stage, the juggling tent, the food vendors' white
  structure, the dip station and the entrance archway; the barn, cabins,
  lodges and the year-round building stay plain blocks with the right roof.
- **b. The arrival first.** Spend the detail on the year-round building,
  the check-in canopy and the loop, because that is what the player sees
  first.
- **c. Free text (expected).** One line each on what the showcase stage,
  the juggling tent, the food structure, the barn and the main building
  actually look like (colour, roof, open or closed sides), and anything
  the interview missed.
