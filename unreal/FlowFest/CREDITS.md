## Characters, motion, and flow props

Characters come from the existing Flow Arts Composer deployed avatar catalog in `@austencloud/scene-3d`, using the optimized source assets under `static/models/avatars/_optimized`. Source materials and textures are retained. Locomotion comes from `static/animations/locomotion-pack`. Existing source asset rights and attribution continue to apply; conversion does not assign a new license to those assets.

The legacy flow animations and the A/B routine for each of 41 prop families are sampled from Flow Arts Composer's production `LiveSequencePerformer3D` and character rig. Compact prop tracks include captured wrist anchors for native prop alignment. The mounted pose comes from `FlowFestEucMountedPoseRig`. Native props are converted from the source prop library's authored models and procedural geometry. The electric-unicycle mesh reproduces `FlowFestElectricUnicycle`. Character model conversion, bone-name normalization, animation retargeting, and texture conversion are performed by the included tools.

Unreal Engine remains subject to Epic's applicable terms.

## Site and authored dressing

The terrain, orthophotography, tree placements, paths, and campsite layout are exported from the existing Flow Fest source dataset. Ground textures reuse the source repository's `static/textures/forest-floor` assets. Tents, canopies, the gatehouse, fire pit, benches, and signposts are authored by the included Blender script. Fire materials are generated by the included Unreal Python script.

Grass prototypes come from the source scene's `static/models/flow-fest-sim/ecology/forest-grass-prototypes.glb`. Damp sedge and hazel understory come from `static/models/forest/ground-life/damp-sedge-tussock.glb` and `woodland-hazel-shrub.glb`. The exported placements, wind response, and ground treatment adapt the browser scene's authored ecology. These assets remain subject to their existing source rights; native conversion assigns no new license.

Six mono ambient loops are original procedural synthesis generated by `Tools/prepare-audio.py`. They use no downloaded recordings or third-party audio library.
